Rating 4 out of 5

As a part-time commuter student with a part-time job, a lot of my hours
are spent driving to campus and heading straight to work. I really
appreciate the flexibility that SDSU has with their class scheduling, so
that way I can still have enough hours for my outside extracurriculars.
I would really like to see a change in social events and clubs that are
active on campus. A lot of the time, I cannot make it to meeting times
and I cannot get as involved in the campus life as I would like because
I am a commuter student with a very packed schedule. I wish that SDSU
would take the time out to consider students like me and change meeting
times or dates to accommodate everyone, not just students who live on
campus.

professors are accessible, friends are from around the country. He loves
college. He's eager to study abroad - everyone seems to do this and he's
all in. Had the option to go to UCSD (free tuition, so that was tough to

the school's career/internship program. Classes are hard but

club and has time to explore San Diego's food scene. Students are smart
and driven. Some balance Greek life, he opted to not do this and has a

Rating 4 out of 5

My overall experience at San Diego State University has been very
positive. I appreciate the vibrant campus life, the diversity of
students, and the wide range of academic programs offered. The
professors are knowledgeable and approachable, and I have found many
opportunities to engage in hands-on learning and research. I
particularly enjoy the sense of community on campus and the resources
available for student success, including tutoring centers, career
services, and student organizations. If I could see one area improve, it
would be the class registration process. Some popular classes fill up
quickly, making it challenging to get into required courses.
Additionally, more on-campus study spaces would be helpful, especially
during peak hours. Overall, SDSU provides a supportive and stimulating
environment for students, and I feel that it has prepared me well for
both academic and professional growth.
