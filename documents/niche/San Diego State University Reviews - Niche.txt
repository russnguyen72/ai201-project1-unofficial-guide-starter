Rating 5 out of 5

I have really enjoyed my experience at San Diego State University so
far. The campus has a great environment and I have met a lot of
supportive classmates and professors who genuinely want students to
succeed. I feel happy and proud to be part of the school community.
There are always activities, events, and opportunities to get involved
which makes the experience more fun and memorable.

One thing I would like to see improve is class scheduling. Sometimes
classes that students need overlap in timing which can make it stressful
to plan semesters and stay on track for graduation. Having more flexible
scheduling or additional class sections would really help students
balance their coursework better.

Overall, my experience has been very positive and SDSU has helped me
grow both academically and personally.
